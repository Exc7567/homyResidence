# Homy Properties: Points of Interest (POI) Data

## About this file (instructions for the AI agent)

- This file lists the approved Points of Interest (POI) near each of the 4 Homy kost/homestay properties, for use on the landing page.
- Audience: *anak kost* (boarding-house residents: students and young workers).
- 🏍️ = travel time **by motorcycle**.
- Times are **estimates** for typical off-peak traffic and may be longer in rush hour. They are not live Google Maps values.
- Every "Route" link opens Google Maps directions from the property pin to the POI in motorcycle mode (`travelmode=two-wheeler`). Use these links as-is.
- Names are kept in their original Indonesian form (e.g. *Universitas*, *Swalayan*, *RS*).
- Each section is self-contained. Do not mix POIs between properties.
